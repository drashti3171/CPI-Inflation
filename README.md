## 📌 Project Overview

Inflation affects the purchasing power of households and the cost of everyday goods and services. The **Consumer Price Index (CPI)** helps track changes in price levels over time.

In this project, I analyzed CPI data for **Rural, Urban, and Rural+Urban** sectors using Microsoft Excel. I prepared and reshaped the data, explored overall index trends, compared selected expenditure categories, calculated percentage changes, and created charts to communicate the results.

The workbook contains the original data, cleaned data, an unpivoted dataset, multiple insight worksheets, and a dashboard.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze CPI index trends over time.
- Compare CPI patterns across Rural, Urban, and Rural+Urban sectors.
- Explore category-level movements in food, fuel, health, education, transport, and other expenditure groups.
- Calculate and compare percentage changes between periods.
- Present findings through Excel charts and a dashboard.
- Practise data cleaning, transformation, analysis, and insight communication.

## 📈 Key Analysis Areas

### 1. CPI trends by sector
Compared the General Index across Rural, Urban, and Rural+Urban sectors to examine how the index changed over time.

### 2. Food category movements
Explored categories such as cereals, milk and products, fruits, vegetables, pulses, oils and fats, and spices.

### 3. Expenditure category comparisons
Compared selected categories, including:
- Food and beverages
- Fuel and light
- Household goods and services
- Health
- Education
- Transport and communication

### 4. Percentage-change analysis
Used period-to-period percentage-change calculations to understand whether a category's index increased or decreased.

A common calculation used for percentage change is:

\[
\text{Percentage Change}=\frac{\text{Current Value}-\text{Previous Value}}{\text{Previous Value}}\times100
\]

### 5. Correlation analysis
The workbook includes correlation calculations for selected pairs of category changes. Correlation indicates how two series move together in the analyzed data; it does **not** by itself prove that one category causes changes in another.

## 💡 Findings Recorded in the Workbook

The workbook's insight notes highlight the following patterns:

- **Sector comparison:** The notes report that the CPI increased year over year across all three sectors from 2016 to 2023, with a comparatively strong increase in the Rural sector in 2022.
- **Food categories:** The notes identify spices and cereals as categories with notable increases between 2022 and 2023, while vegetables and oils and fats show decreases over the comparison described.
- **Category variation:** The notes report differing levels of fluctuation across food and beverages, health, and household goods and services.

These are summaries of the workbook's written insight notes. Before presenting them as final findings, verify the comparison periods, percentage calculations, and underlying values in the worksheets.
